How the numbers were made

The tool

A rules-based Python classifier (standard library, no LLM, no network). It scores 10 causes from ledger fields and from phrases in the customer's replies, newest reply weighted 2x. If a customer replied and nothing matched, it says Needs review instead of guessing.

The data

20 synthetic overdue invoices with hand-written email threads (the holdout set). Names end in "(synthetic)". No real customer, invoice or person is used. Results as of .

Accuracy

Independent proof of work for Alder (meetalder.com). Not Alder's product, code or data. Drafts are never sent.
